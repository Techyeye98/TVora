# TVora visual identity

Graphite #0A1116, mint #8CE8CA, solid surface #111D25, secondary surface #172731.
System sans-serif with bold display type, rounded rectangular actions and original vector artwork.
The V mark doubles as a personal-choice check inside a TV outline. Tagline: Your TV. Your way.
The welcome screen is type-first; the dashboard uses device and action cards; Home studio groups
app arrangement, visibility, appearance and quick buttons. Large text gets a single-column grid.
TV launcher appearance remains the chosen original coloured-glow theme.
