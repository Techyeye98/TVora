CH 03How it works

Four steps from your sofa

Phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi. TVora walks you through every screen with steps for your TV's brand.

  1. STEP 1

    Let the phone in

    Turn on debugging in the TV's Developer options. When the TV asks to trust your phone, tick Always allow and choose OK. It's the only fiddly part, and it takes about a minute.

  2. STEP 2

    Review the plan

    TVora lists every app it would switch off and everything it keeps. Change anything you like under Customise, or tap Make it clean.

  3. STEP 3

    Make it clean

    A restore point is saved on your phone first. Then the clutter is switched off and the new home screen goes in. Put the phone down; it buzzes when it needs you.

  4. STEP 4

    Check it on the TV

    Press Home on the remote and confirm you can see it. If you don't answer, TVora puts the old home screen back on its own.

CH 05Restore

Everything can be put back

TVora records every change it makes, so undo is exact: each app goes back to the state it was in, not simply switched on.

0/112

Every original package matched its baseline after a full clean-up, restore and reboot on a Mi TV (MiTV-AXSO0, Android 9). The TV's own home screen came back on its own.

Restore covers the changes TVora recorded. It isn't a backup of app data, logins, firmware or changes made before you used TVora.

  • A restore point before the first changeSaved on your phone. Undo everything from it in one go.
  • Put back one app at a timeThe Switched off list brings back any single app.
  • Save a copy outside the appExport a zip with recovery scripts to anywhere you choose.
  • It stops when something looks wrongIf the TV stops answering mid-change, you choose to carry on or undo. Nothing else changes until then.
  • Check for leftovers after updatesA TV software update can switch things back on. TVora finds them and switches them off again.
CH 06Privacy

Between your phone and your TV

TVora has no account, analytics, advertising or server of its own. Your TV records and restore points stay in the app's private storage on your phone.

You approve every change

Your phone's screen lock confirms it's you before TVora changes anything, and a live picture from the TV confirms it's your TV.

Pairing stays private

Pairing codes are used once and never saved. The phone's key lives in Android Keystore, and Reset my key clears it.

Your photo goes to one place

A wallpaper photo is resized, stripped of its metadata and sent only to the TV you picked. The update check reads TVora's public GitHub releases.

    CH 08Questions

    Questions

    How do I install TVora?
    1. On your Android phone, open the latest release.
    2. Under Assets, tap the file ending in .apk. You don't need a GitHub account.
    3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
    4. Put your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi, open TVora and tap Start.
    What do I need?

    An Android phone running Android 9 or newer, and an Android TV or Google TV on the same trusted Wi-Fi network. No computer, account or subscription.

    Does TVora delete apps from my TV?

    No. It switches apps off, which puts them to sleep. They stay installed, and you can put any of them back from the Switched off list or undo everything from the restore point.

    Why does it need debugging turned on?

    Debugging over Wi-Fi is how a phone is allowed to change settings on the TV, and the TV asks you to approve your phone first. When you tap Finish, TVora switches debugging off again unless you chose to keep it on.

    Newer TVs use Wireless debugging with a six-digit pairing code. TVora handles that too.

    What happens if something goes wrong halfway?

    TVora stops and tells you what happened. You choose to carry on with the rest or undo what was done. If the new home screen doesn't take over, the old one is put back automatically.

    Will it work on my Samsung or LG TV?

    No. Samsung uses Tizen and LG uses webOS, and TVora works with Android TV and Google TV. Some TVs, such as Fire TV and some TCL models, get the clean-up but keep their own home screen.

    Is TVora free?

    Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads and no in-app purchases. If it helps you, a star on GitHub is welcome.
