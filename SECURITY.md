# Security

TVora is a community Android TV utility with device-tested cleanup and restore flows.
Functional testing does not constitute an independent security audit.
Do not post ADB keys, pairing codes, private photos, signing keys or full device backups in issues.
Use the in-app redacted report and review it before sharing. Public issue reports should describe
the problem without credentials or exploit details. For private security reports, contact Namya Mehta on [Telegram @Techyeye](https://t.me/Techyeye).
Contact the maintainer first before sharing sensitive details. Do not send TVora reports to the
upstream maintainers as though this were their release.

Only connect to TVs you own or are authorized to manage, on a trusted local network. TV permissions
must be granted by their owner. Disable debugging when management is finished.
